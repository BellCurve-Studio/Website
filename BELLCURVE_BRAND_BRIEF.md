# BellCurve Studio — Logo & Brand Direction

## Overview

BellCurve is a sharp, independent digital product and software studio. The brand should feel like a thoughtful technical partner for ambitious teams—not a generic software agency, corporate consultancy, or futuristic AI startup.

## Brand personality

- Intelligent, calm, and decisive
- Editorial rather than overly technical
- Human and warm, but structurally rigorous
- Modern craftsmanship with visible personality
- Clear, direct, and quietly confident

## Core idea

“BellCurve” represents a meaningful shift: moving teams from friction, complexity, and average outcomes toward clarity, momentum, and better systems.

The logo should suggest:

- A positive inflection point
- Progress and transformation
- Precision with a human touch
- A studio that makes complex digital work feel clearer

Avoid literal charts, generic trend arrows, stock “tech” icons, or obvious AI imagery.

## Visual language from the website

The website combines:

- Warm paper-like backgrounds
- Dark technical ink
- Signal orange for momentum and action
- Soft blue, mint, and gold as supporting tones
- Editorial serif moments paired with bold modern sans-serif typography
- Rounded cards, strong dark outlines, offset shadows, and subtle geometric orbit lines

The logo should belong in this world: tactile, clean, and confident.

## Logo direction

Create a primary wordmark plus a standalone symbol.

### Wordmark

- `BELLCURVE` should be the primary expression.
- Prefer a bold, compact, modern sans-serif wordmark.
- It should feel strong and engineered, with tight spacing and a slightly editorial character.
- `STUDIO` can be a small secondary descriptor in a mono or restrained sans-serif style.
- The wordmark should work in technical ink, white/cream, and orange-accent versions.

### Symbol / icon

The icon should be simple enough to work as:

- Navbar/favicon-style mark
- Social avatar
- Browser icon
- App icon
- Small badge on cards and documents

Potential concepts:

- A custom abstract `B` built from a curve or inflection
- A bell curve interrupted by a decisive upward shift
- A single-point curve representing the move from complexity to clarity
- A circular or rounded mark with a technical, geometric `B`

The current UI uses a small circular orange `B` mark. The final symbol can evolve this into something more distinctive, but should remain recognisable at very small sizes.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical ink | Deep navy-charcoal | `#17232D` |
| Paper / primary background | Warm cream | `#F4EFE5` |
| Light surface | Soft ivory | `#FFFDF8` |
| Signal accent | Coral orange | `#ED542D` |
| Energy accent | Warm gold | `#FFBD5F` |
| Supporting blue | Powder blue | `#D7E4FF` |
| Supporting green | Soft mint | `#CBE9DA` |

The logo should primarily use technical ink on cream, or cream/white on technical ink. Coral orange should be a memorable accent—not the only usable version.

## Typography direction

- Primary: bold, highly legible geometric/grotesk sans-serif
- Accent: expressive editorial serif, ideally italic
- Utility labels: compact monospace

The logo should not use a thin, delicate typeface or a stereotypical futuristic font.

## Do / don’t

### Do

- Make it memorable at small sizes
- Prioritize a distinctive silhouette
- Balance structured geometry with one expressive curve
- Design for ink-on-paper first, then digital use
- Create horizontal, stacked, icon-only, and single-color variants

### Don’t

- Use gradients, glass effects, or 3D rendering
- Use generic code brackets, rockets, lightning bolts, or neural-network symbols
- Make it look like a finance, analytics, or medical brand
- Overcomplicate the bell-curve metaphor
- Rely on tiny details that disappear in a favicon

## Requested deliverables

- Primary horizontal logo
- Stacked logo
- Icon / monogram mark
- Light and dark variants
- One-color variants
- Favicon/app-icon version
- SVG, PDF, PNG, and editable vector source
- Basic usage sheet with spacing, minimum size, and color guidance

## Designer creative direction

> Design BellCurve Studio as an editorial software studio: technical ink on warm paper, with one confident coral signal. The mark should capture a positive shift or inflection—not a literal graph—feeling intelligent, crafted, human, and built for momentum.
